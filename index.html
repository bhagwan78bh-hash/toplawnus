Essentials

Six habits of great gardens

Adopt these simple routines to build resilient grass, nourish soil biology, and maintain a vibrant landscape with less effort.

💧 Hydration

Water deeply, not daily

Soak the soil 2–3 times a week so roots grow deep. Water early in the morning to cut evaporation and fungus.

🌱 Soil Biology

Feed the soil

Mix in compost each season. Healthy soil holds moisture, feeds plants and reduces the need for fertilizer.

✂️ Turf Care

Mow high

Keep grass at 3–4 inches and never remove more than a third of the blade. Taller grass shades out weeds.

🍂 Root Shield

Mulch generously

A 2–3 inch layer of mulch locks in moisture, keeps roots cool and suppresses weeds. Keep it off plant stems.

🐝 Ecosystem

Invite pollinators

Plant native flowers in clusters and skip broad-spectrum pesticides to attract bees, butterflies and helpful insects.

🔍 Early Detection

Check often

A weekly walk-through helps you catch pests, disease and thirsty plants before they become real problems.

Seasonal Calendar

What to do each season

Keep your lawn and garden synchronized with nature's rhythm using these straightforward seasonal benchmarks.

🌱 Spring Wake-Up

Spring

  • Test your soil chemistry & pH
  • Rake and overseed bare patches
  • Sow cool-season veggies
  • Divide perennials & edge beds
☀️ Summer Vigor

Summer

  • Water deeply in early morning
  • Deadhead flowers for more blooms
  • Mulch to retain soil moisture
  • Mow higher to resist heat stress
🍁 Autumn Prep

Autumn

  • Aerate and overseed the lawn
  • Plant hardy spring bulbs
  • Compost fallen leaves
  • Apply fall slow-release fertilizer
❄️ Winter Rest

Winter

  • Clean, oil, and sharpen tools
  • Prune dormant trees and shrubs
  • Protect tender plants from frost
  • Plan next year's garden beds
💡
Pro tip: Take a photo of your garden every month from the exact same viewpoint. Looking back shows what thrived, what struggled, and gives you clear insight on where to plant next.
